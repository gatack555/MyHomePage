あなたの試合を、棋譜にする。

“なんとなく”を、具体的な形に。

答えではなく、気づきを。

Pongorithmは、卓球の得点パターンと失点パターンを見える化するアプリです。 どの技で点を取れているのか、どのコースに決まっているのか、 相手のミスなのか、自分の決定打なのか。 お渡しするのは、答えではなく「棋譜」。 そこから次の一手を読むのは、あなた自身です。

「このコースのサーブは得点に結びつきやすい」 「このコースにツッツキすると相手がミスしやすい」 強い選手は、試合の最中に自分で分析して、そこを突いてくる。

でも初心者は、目の前の一球に必死で、そこまで頭が回らない。

Pongorithmは、上級者が試合中に考えていそうなことを、コース別・技別に見える化します。 くり返すうちに、コート上でも、あなたが”自分の頭”で気づけるようになる。 アプリが答えを出すのではなく、あなた自身が分析できるように。

Concept

Turn every match into a game you can study, point by point.

Turn a vague hunch into something you can see.

Not answers, but insights.

Pongorithm is an app that visualizes how you win and lose points in table tennis. Which strokes are earning your points? Where are your winning shots landing? Are you scoring off your opponent’s mistakes, or your own winners?

What Pongorithm gives you isn’t the answer—it’s a record of your match. Reading it, and choosing your next move, is up to you.

“Serves to this spot tend to win points.” “When I push to this spot, my opponent tends to miss.” Strong players spot patterns like these in the middle of a match and turn them to their advantage.

Beginners, though, are usually too locked onto the ball in front of them to think that far ahead.

Pongorithm visualizes the kinds of patterns experienced players are watching for during a match, broken down by placement and stroke. Over time, you’ll start catching them yourself—even out on the court.

The app isn’t here to give you the answers. It’s here to teach you to analyze the game yourself.

Record Your Match

  • While watching a match live
    • Opening Mode — record the first three shots, starting from the serve
    • Ending Mode — record the final three shots that win or lose the point
  • While reviewing match video later
    • Full Rally Mode — record every shot in the rally

Build Your Table Tennis IQ

  • Replay your rally records and review every point
  • Step back and read your rallies to see the patterns behind points won and lost
  • Plan how you’ll win the next point

Win or lose, there’s always something to discover.
